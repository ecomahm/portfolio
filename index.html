The thinking behind the winners

Anyone can show an ad that worked. Here's why it worked, so you can judge whether the thinking carries over to your brand.

Birra Montecassino · Premium beer · Loom breakdown, 2:59

Selling a beer that costs twice as much as anything else in UK supermarkets

Top spender since launchPremium positioningUnique mechanism

The research

Customer research on how big beer is made: treated water and force-pumped CO2. That gave me the contrast for the whole ad.

The angle

Lead with the price instead of hiding it. The ad opens on the claim that it costs 2x more than anything else at UK supermarkets, borrowed from a pet-niche ad that sells out every month.

The mechanism

Brewed authentically in Italy with water from a real spring. The yeast releases its own CO2, so the carbonation is slower and natural, which makes it easier on your stomach.

The result

The highest-spending ad in the account since the day it launched.
